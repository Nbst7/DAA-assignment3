## dsu empty graph (dsu)

## dsu single vertex loop (dsu)

- **Why:** Union of a vertex with itself changes nothing: 1 component of size 1.
- **Input:** `{"task":"dsu","n":1,"operations":[[0,0]]}`
- **Expected:** `{"states":[[1,1]]}`

## dsu loop and repeated union (dsu)

- **Why:** 0-1 merges (3 comps, max 2); 1-2 merges (2 comps, max 3); 0-2 and 3-3 do nothing.
- **Input:** `{"task":"dsu","n":4,"operations":[[0,1],[1,2],[0,2],[3,3]]}`
- **Expected:** `{"states":[[3,2],[2,3],[2,3],[2,3]]}`

## dsu mixed merges (dsu)
